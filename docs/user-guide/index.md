1. **[Start Here](quick-start.md)** - Install and verify setup
2. **[Convert Adventures](converting-content.md#adventures)** - Transform your adventures into PDFs
3. **[Troubleshoot Issues](troubleshooting.md)** - Resolve common LaTeX and content problems

### Spell & Creature References
Ideal for creating custom reference documents:

1. **[Content Conversion](converting-content.md#spells-and-creatures)** - Learn filtering and organization
2. **[CLI Reference](cli-reference.md#filtering-options)** - Master advanced filtering options

## What You Can Create

- **Adventure Modules**: Complete adventures with maps, NPCs, and encounters
- **Spell Compendiums**: Organized collections of spells with filtering options
- **Monster Manuals**: Creature stat blocks with professional formatting
- **Reference Documents**: Custom compilations of rules, items, and more
- **Homebrew Publications**: Your own content with professional presentation

## Before You Begin

!!! info "Prerequisites"
    - **Python 3.12+** installed on your system
    - **Basic command line** familiarity (we'll guide you through it)
    - **LaTeX distribution** (automatically handled in most cases)

!!! tip "New to Command Line?"
    Don't worry! Our [Quick Start Guide](quick-start.md) includes step-by-step instructions with screenshots and assumes no prior command line experience.
