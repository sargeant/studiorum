% Inline (non-floating) image block
% Usage: \StudiorumImageInline[<width fraction>]{file}{caption}[label]
\NewDocumentCommand{\StudiorumImageInline}{ s o m m o }{%%
  \par\medskip
  \noindent\centering
  \IfNoValueTF{#2}{%%
    \includegraphics[width=\linewidth,height=0.45\textheight,keepaspectratio]{#3}%%
  }{%%
    \includegraphics[width=#2\linewidth,height=0.45\textheight,keepaspectratio]{#3}%%
  }%%
  \IfBooleanF{#1}{%%
    \par\smallskip
    \captionof{figure}{#4}%%
    \IfNoValueF{#5}{\label{#5}}%%
  }%%
  \par\medskip
}

% Standard floating figure
% Usage: \StudiorumImageFloat[<width fraction>]{file}{caption}[label]

    }%%
    \IfBooleanF{#1}{%%
      \caption{#4}%%
      \IfNoValueF{#5}{\label{#5}}%%
    }%%
  \end{figure}%%
}

% Full-page (dedicated page; fits within text block)

    }%%

}

% Full-page bleed (fills the physical page; may distort aspect ratio)
% Usage: \StudiorumImageFullpageBleed{file}{caption}[label]
\NewDocumentCommand{\StudiorumImageFullpageBleed}{ s o m m o }{%%

    \IfBooleanF{#1}{%%
      \caption{#4}%%
      \IfNoValueF{#5}{\label{#5}}%%
    }%%
  \end{figure}
  \clearpage
}

The party finds themselves in a small village plagued by mysterious events.

A quiet farming community with a dark secret.

The village of Testbrook sits nestled in a peaceful valley, surrounded by rolling hills and ancient oak trees.

This small settlement serves as the starting point for many testing adventures, providing a safe haven for adventurers.

\setcounter{chapter}{1}
\chapter{The Repository}\label{ch:001}

The ancient repository holds forgotten knowledge and dangerous artifacts.

Strange magical energies permeate the structure.

\subsection{Approaching the Repository}

The Repository of Bugs appears as a crystalline structure that seems to shift and flicker between different architectural styles.

Strange energy patterns dance across its surface, and the air around it hums with barely contained power.

\subsection{Interior Chambers}

The main entrance displays scrolling text in various programming languages.

Shimmering energy fields test visitors' knowledge and intent.

Glowing crystals show the current stability level of the repository.

\subsection{The Core}

At the heart of the repository lies the Core, a swirling vortex of pure logic and data.

This is where the party must restore stability to prevent a catastrophic system failure.
