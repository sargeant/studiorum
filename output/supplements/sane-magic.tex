\section{Summoning Items}
Summoning items give their users shiny new friends to help them destroy their enemies or solve problems. With the exception of the Onyx dog, the Silver Raven and the Ivory Goat of Terror, the value of these items is heavily dependent on how expensive mercenaries are in your campaign world. The basic rules makes hiring even quite competent allies startlingly cheap, and while these items aren't based on the ridiculous 2 gp/day rule, they do assume that hiring combat ready minions is a relatively cheap activity that most players will be doing a little bit of if the situation warrants it. Adjust the below prices accordingly if this is not true in your campaign world.

\section{Items that Won't be priced}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textit{Candle of Invocation}: Because we've learned a thing or two from 3.5
\item \textit{Deck of Many Things}: Because lolrandom
\item \textit{Efreeti Bottle}: Because \textit{Wish}
\item \textit{Iron flask}: Because \textit{Wish}
\item \textit{Ring of Three Wishes}: Because \textit{Wish}
\item \textit{Luck Blade}: Because \textit{Wish}
\item \textit{Well of Many Worlds}: Because if the GM wants you to have a TARDIS you'll have one and if they don't you won't
\item \textit{Wand of Wonder}: Because lolrandom
\item \textit{Ring of Djinni Summoning}: It's pretty much everything from the gamechanging list in a single item. I can't see anyone selling these.
\item \textit{Bag of Tricks}: Creating permanent living creatures does absolutely silly things to the economy.
\item \textit{Tome of the Stilled Tongue}: No wizard would ever sell this.
\item Manuals and tomes +2: If these come up, the GM is probably giving them to the whole party as a "level up" type thing
\item \textit{Belt of Giant Strength}: Breaks bounded Accuracy
\item \textit{Potion of Giant Strength}: Breaks bounded accuracy
\item \textit{Rod of Resurrection}: Plot device
\item \textit{Helm of Brilliance}: Lolrandom
\item \textit{Bag of Beans}: Lolrandom (Seriously, it summons mummy lords with treasure filled crypts)
\item \textit{Staff of the Magi}: Too much power
\item \textit{Manual of Golems}: This is a blueprint, not an item. Use whatever rules go into pricing blueprints for your campaign and adjust the golem crafting prices as needed.
\item Cursed items
\end{itemize}
