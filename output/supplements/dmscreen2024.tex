\subsection{Death Saving Throws}
When you start your turn with 0 HP, roll 1d20, and you succeed on a 10+.


\begin{description}
\item[Third Success.]
You become Stable.

\item[Third Failure.]
You die.

\item[Rolling a 1.]
Counts as two failures.

\item[Rolling a 20.]
You regain 1 Hit Point.

\end{description}

If you take damage at 0 HP, it counts as a failed Death Saving Throw or two failures if the damage is from a Critical Hit.
