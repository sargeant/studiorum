\DndItemHeader{Act of Small Kindness}{Very Rare (requires attunement by a sorcerer and wizard)}

This flower radiates a gentle aura of warmth and reassurance. Upon holding it you are reminded of ancient words of commitment and love. Though often overlooked in favor of more ostentatious treasures, the Act of Small Kindness has the power to mend hearts and bolster resolve when wielded with sincerity.

After you attune to the Act of Small Kindness, you gain the following benefits:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Aura of Support} (Passive): While attuned, the bearer gains a +2 bonus to Wisdom (Insight) and Charisma (Persuasion) checks when offering emotional support.
    \item \textbf{Joint Decision} (Bonus Action): Once per long rest, the bearer can invoke this ability to negotiate terms on any life decision, large or small, with advantage on all relevant checks.
	\item \textbf{Shoulder to Cry On} (Reaction): When an ally within 10 feet experiences disappointment or frustration, you can expend a charge to provide comforting words or a hastily prepared snack. This grants them temporary hit points equal to your level plus your Charisma modifier.
\end{itemize}

\subparagraph{Bound by Vow}

The Act of Small Kindness cannot be discarded, forgotten, or out-leveled. If neglected for too long, it will gently remind the bearer of their promises by mysteriously appearing on their pillow or in the pocket of their adventuring gear.
